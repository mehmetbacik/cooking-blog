## Ingredients
- 2 cups flour  
- 1 ½ cups sugar  
- ¾ cup cocoa powder  
- 2 eggs  
- 1 cup milk  
- ½ cup butter  

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 180°C.  
2. Mix all ingredients until smooth.  
3. Pour into a pan and bake for 30–35 minutes.  
4. Let it cool and add frosting on top.
